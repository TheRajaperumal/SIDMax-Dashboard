The Problem With Traditional Detailing

For decades, structural drafters and detailers have faced a painfully repetitive workflow. Drawing individual rebars, manually calculating lap lengths, visually counting lines for Bar Bending Schedules, and constantly realigning text after revisions. A single architectural change can trigger hours of tedious, error-prone rework.

SIDMax changes the paradigm. Built by engineers for engineers, SIDMax acts as a smart layer on top of your existing CAD environment. It understands structural elements—it doesn't just see lines and text; it sees reinforcement arrays, dowels, and boundaries. By automating the mechanical aspects of drafting, SIDMax allows detailers to focus on what actually matters: structural integrity and design intent.

Why Choose SIDMax?

⚡

Massive Time Reduction

Turn days of tedious drafting into hours. Tasks like placing continuous elevation tags, generating rebar arrays across boundaries, or calculating complex lap lengths are reduced to a single click.

🎯

Zero Calculation Errors

Eliminate human error. SIDMax's built-in calculators automatically total lengths, convert Imperial to Metric on the fly, and ensure your bar quantities are perfectly accurate every single time.

🤖

Smart Automation

Don't just copy and paste. Use our intelligent tools to automatically sort text vertically, swap labels, generate sequential numbering, and instantly replace text blocks without breaking formatting.
